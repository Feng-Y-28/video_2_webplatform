# Rubric A — content (principle.md)

Score 1–5. 5 = nothing to change · 3 = usable with one clear fix · 1 = wrong or missing.

| id | item | blocking | what 5 looks like |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | Faithful to the source | blocking | The mechanism and numbers say what the paper / sources say; no overclaim, no result attributed to the wrong cause. |
| A2 | One idea | blocking | A single mechanism, written as cause → effect in ≤ 3 steps. Everything else is under *Left out*. |
| A3 | Proof numbers sourced and computable | blocking | 2–4 numbers, each with a section/table/URL or labelled "simplified example"; derived numbers follow from the inputs. |
| A4 | Problem a viewer can feel | — | The problem is stated in everyday terms that the stated audience understands without background. |
| A5 | Visualisable | — | The mechanism can be shown as objects that move or change; no step that is only words. |
| A6 | Depth fits audience | — | Neither shallow (a slogan) nor a lecture; terms needed are ≤ 4. |
| A7 | Sensible cuts | — | Nothing on the *Left out* list is needed to understand the mechanism. |
